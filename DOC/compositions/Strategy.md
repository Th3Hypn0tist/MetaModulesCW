# Strategy composition family

Strategy follows the same MetaModule Composition model as operational domains.

It does not require a separate duplicate business datastore.

Strategy tools operate over existing canonical information such as:

- Project
- Plan
- Measurement
- Assessment
- Identity
- Asset
- Contract
- RuleSet
- other relevant Universals.

## Example methods

- SWOT
- PESTEL
- OKR
- Balanced Scorecard
- Business Model Canvas
- Porter Five Forces
- Scenario Planning
- Decision Matrix
- Risk Matrix
- Roadmap

## Example capabilities

- Board
- Portfolio
- Decision
- Due Diligence / M&A
- Governance / GRC
- Vendor
- Tender / RFP
- Benefits
- War Room
- Investor / Management Reporting
- Management System

## Role-oriented views

C-suite views are custom Projections based on role or job context, not separate canonical Strategy modules.

Examples:

- CEO
- CFO
- COO
- CTO/CIO
- CHRO
- CSO

A War Room is also a Projection over canonical truth rather than an independent authoritative store.
